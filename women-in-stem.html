Women in STEM

Women who excel in the STEM fields: science, technology, engineering, or mathematics

  • Ada Lovelace, the first computer programmer
  • Amelia Earhart, American aviator and author.
  • Cecilia Payne, British-American astronomer and astrophysicist.
  • Dorothy Vaughan, American mathematician and computer programmer.
  • Gladys West, American mathematician related to the development of GPS
  • Grace Hopper, American computer scientist and Navy Rear Admiral.
  • Hedy Lamarr, Austrian-American actress and inventor, pioneer in the development of wi-fi
  • Hypatia of Alexandria, philosopher, mathematician, and astronomer.
  • Katherine Johnson, American mathematician and physicist.
  • Mae Jemison, American astronaut and physician.
  • Marie Curie, Polish-French physicist and chemist, the first woman to be a Nobel laureate
  • Marie Pasteur, French chemist and microbiologist.
  • Mileva Maric, Serbian physicist.
  • Rosalind Franklin, British chemist and X-ray crystallographer.
  • Sally Ride, American astronaut and physicist.
  • Tu Youyou, Chinese pharmaceutical chemist and Nobel Laureate in Physiology or Medicine.
  • Vera Rubin, American astronomer
