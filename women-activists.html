Women Activists

Women who have been politically and socially active to make the world a better place

  • Audre Lorde, American feminist poet, writer, and activist
  • Aung San Suu Kyi, Burmese politician and Nobel Peace Prize laureate
  • Conceição Evaristo, Brazilian essayist, writer, poet, researcher
  • Dian Fossey, American primatologist who studied gorillas in Rwanda
  • Emmeline Pankhurst, suffragette who led the fight for women's suffrage in the UK.
  • Greta Thunberg, Swedish climate activist
  • Irene Khan, Bangladeshi-British lawyer and human rights activist
  • Jane Goodall, English primatologist known for her work on animal behavior.
  • Lélia Gonzalez, Brazilian anthropologist, feminist, woman rights activist, politician
  • Leymah Gbowee, Liberian peace activist and Nobel Peace Prize laureate
  • Malala Yousafzai, Pakistani activist for female education and the youngest Nobel Prize laureate
  • María Elena Moyano, Peruvian social activist assassinated by the Shining Path
  • Maria Ressa, Filipino-American journalist fighting for press freedom
  • Marina Silva, Brazilian environmental activist and Minister of Environment
  • Maya Angelou, American poet, playwright, novelist, and activist.
  • Nadia Murad, Iraqi Yazidi human rights activist and survivor of the Yazidi genocide
  • Naomi Klein, Canadian author of books on globalization and climate change.
  • Narges Mohammadi, Iranian human rights activist and Nobel Peace Prize laureate
  • Pagu, Brazilian poet, playwright, and activist.
  • Rigoberta Menchú Tum, Guatemalan indigenous activist and Nobel Peace Prize laureate.
  • Rosa Parks, American civil rights activist and key figure in the Montgomery bus boycott.
  • Shirin Ebadi, Iranian human rights lawyer and Nobel Peace Prize laureate
  • Simone de Beauvoir, French philosopher and feminist
  • Susan Sarandon, American actress and activist.
  • Wangari Maathai, Kenyan environmental activist and Nobel Peace Prize laureate.
